# Incremental Dynamic Earnings Intelligence 9.5

This resumes the merged 98/200 checkpoint. Local primary-evidence checks support 103 historical models and four issuer-guidance-backed forward models, with no prior supported names lost. Five historical recoveries are CNI, EOG, WOW.AX, COL.AX and TLS.AX. This is not a claim that the remaining 97 models are complete.

CNI's 40-F incorporates audited annual financial statements filed as a 6-K. Only that reviewed issuer/CIK gains the additional form allowance; annual duration and currency checks remain active. EOG cash spending combines two disjoint cash-flow rows with matching currency, interval and accession. Business acquisitions are separate.

Woolworths, Coles and Telstra use reproducible, hash-bound PDF adapters. The shared PDF reader now handles rotated pages and duplicate text outside the actual year header. Each figure retains its PDF page, literal, scale, source URL, document hash and fiscal dates. Cash spending includes explicitly disclosed capitalized intangibles, lease principal and finance costs classified in financing where applicable. Employee-trust shares are excluded from the denominator. Rounded source share counts remain labelled as rounded. Changed PDF hashes are rejected pending review.

The 9.3 worker now rotates across every original company, including historically supported issuers missing forward evidence. Legacy seven-day delays migrate once: unresolved cases retry in four hours, supported cases in 24 hours, and repeated request failures back off to at most 24 hours. Provider cooldowns remain enforced. Registered PDF adapters participate in the same bounded worker. Other issuers still requiring parser/model development are not described as externally blocked or complete.

Three reviewed forward parsers add Adobe's annual GAAP diluted EPS range, Dell's annual GAAP EPS point and UnitedHealth's annual GAAP diluted EPS floor. Points/floors are held constant across earnings scenarios; differences in valuation multiples are explicit assumptions. No upper earnings forecast is invented for a floor. Adobe's 52/53-week calendar is verified against the annual report. Guidance ending within 90 days is flagged as requiring next-year outlook before a validated pre-recovery ranking. Non-GAAP-only guidance never silently becomes GAAP earnings.

The new session collector requests provider one-minute charts with extended sessions enabled. Samples require an issuer-symbol match, a matching quote currency, a real provider timestamp within the provider's declared session, and no future time. Every sample carries session boundaries, UTC sample/retrieval times, source and freshness. Samples are not executable ticks. Missing sources remain explicit; daily closes are never passed off as pre-market or after-hours prices. The JSON bridge and HTML dashboard expose the extra session fields while preserving existing daily-close valuation inputs. Scan and weekly session reports have separate publishing ownership.

`verify_outputs_95.py` independently inspects existing generated outputs: the frozen 98-name baseline, exact source cash-flow figures, net share counts, forward EPS/multiple arithmetic and session timestamps. The existing 9.2/9.4 verifiers continue to run. All 200, HPE/SKS/DUG entry thresholds, six daily reviews, emergency alerts, discovery, Webull/Kraken and the AUD1,000 trial are preserved. There are no orders or trades.
